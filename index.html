Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Note Memory ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. We believe that your educational data belongs to you.

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal information, location data, or device identifiers.
  • Local Storage: All learning statistics, practice session logs, and user settings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to remote servers.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: The App does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or tracking analytics.
